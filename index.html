01 · Context

Why this audit exists

ARTICLE 4 AI literacy

Under Article 4 of the EU AI Act, providers and deployers of AI systems are required to take measures to support the development of AI literacy among staff and other people dealing with the operation and use of AI systems on their behalf.

The measures should take account of relevant technical knowledge, experience, education and training, the context in which the AI system is used, and the people or groups on whom the system is used. This page is an educational self-audit rather than a legal determination of compliance.

04 · Hygiene

Five-point hygiene check

01
Bias
Have you considered whether the workflow could produce unfair or systematically different outcomes?
02
Privacy
Is personal or confidential information protected and minimised before being entered into the AI system?
03
Consent / permission
Have you checked the relevant permission, notice or lawful basis for the information used in this workflow?
04
Attribution / human ownership
Is there a clearly responsible person who reviews and owns the final output?
05
Transparency
Have you considered whether people need to be informed about the AI's role in this particular workflow?
