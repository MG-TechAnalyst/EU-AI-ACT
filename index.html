01 · Legal context

What Article 4 asks for

Article 4 requires providers and deployers of AI systems to take measures to support the development of AI literacy among their staff and other persons dealing with the operation and use of AI systems on their behalf. The measures should take account of technical knowledge, experience, education and training, the context of use, and the people or groups on whom the systems are used.
Current Article 4 wording reflects the July 2026 amendment. The amended provision does not require organisations to guarantee a specific level of AI literacy for every individual.

04 · Five-point hygiene check

Check the workflow

1. Bias
Have you considered whether the workflow could produce unfair or systematically different outcomes?
2. Privacy
Is personal or confidential information protected and minimised before it is entered into the AI system?
3. Consent / permission
Have you checked the relevant permission, notice or lawful basis for using the information in this workflow?
4. Attribution / human ownership
Is there a clearly responsible person who reviews and owns the final output?
5. Transparency
Have you considered whether people need to be informed about the AI's role in this particular workflow?
